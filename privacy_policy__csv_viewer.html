Privacy Policy for CSV Viewer in Side Panel or New Tab

Last Updated: April 15, 2025

Thank you for using CSV Viewer in Side Panel or New Tab. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience using our extension.

1. Information We Don't Collect

CSV Viewer operates entirely within your browser and does not:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Track your browsing activity
  • Send your CSV data to any external servers
  • Store your data beyond your current browser session
  • Use cookies or any similar tracking technologies

2. How Your Data is Handled

When you upload or drag-and-drop a CSV file into our extension:

  • The file is processed entirely within your browser
  • Data remains on your device and is never transmitted externally
  • No data is retained after you close the extension or clear your browser data

3. Permission Usage

CSV Viewer in Side Panel or New Tab requests the following permission:

  • sidePanel: This permission is solely used to display the CSV Viewer in Side Panel or New Tab interface within Chrome's side panel, allowing you to view and analyze CSV data alongside your browsing experience.

4. Third-Party Services

CSV Viewer does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

5. Children's Privacy

CSV Viewer in Side Panel or New Tab does not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

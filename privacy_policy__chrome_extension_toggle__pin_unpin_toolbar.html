Privacy Policy for Chrome Extension Toggle - Pin/Unpin Toolbar

Last Updated: April 16, 2025

Summary: We collect absolutely no personal data. All extension data is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to your data and it never leaves your device.

Introduction

Thank you for using Chrome Extension Toggle! This privacy policy explains what information our extension collects, how it is used, and the measures we take to protect your privacy.

Information Collection and Use

What information we collect:

Chrome Extension Toggle collects and stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Extension IDs: The extension IDs you select to be part of your toolbar management list
  • Extension state preferences: Whether you want your toolbar extensions to be enabled or disabled
  • No personal information: We do not collect any personal information, browsing history, or other user data

How we use this information:

  • Local storage only: All information is stored locally on your device using Chrome's Storage API
  • Functionality only: This information is used solely to provide the core functionality of the extension (toggling your selected extensions on and off)
  • No data transmission: No data is transmitted to our servers or any third parties

Permissions

Our extension requires the following permissions:

  • Management: To access the list of installed extensions and to enable/disable them based on your preferences
  • Storage: To save your toolbar extension list and preferences locally on your device

Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • No data sharing: We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information with third parties
  • No external servers: Our extension does not communicate with any external servers or services
  • No analytics: We do not use any analytics or tracking services

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted, your information remains secure within Chrome's built-in security model.

Your Rights

As all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can:

  • Clear all stored data by uninstalling the extension
  • Manage your extension preferences through the options page

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the Chrome Web Store listing. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Privacy Policy for Chrome Extension Icon Generator

Last Updated: April 20, 2025

1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing the Chrome Extension Icon Generator ("Extension"). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how your information is handled when you use our Extension.

Icon Generator is committed to protecting your privacy. The Extension is designed to process all data locally within your browser, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information or uploaded images.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Our Extension is designed with privacy in mind. We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any images you upload to the Extension
  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII)
  • We do not collect browsing history or website data
  • We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • We do not collect analytics or usage data

3. Local Processing

All functionality of the Icon Generator Extension operates locally within your browser:
  • Image Processing: When you upload an image to generate icons, all processing occurs entirely on your device.
  • Generated Icons: The icons created by the Extension remain on your device and are only saved when you explicitly choose to download them.
  • No Remote Servers: We do not use any remote servers for image processing or storage.

4. Chrome Permissions

Our Extension requires minimal permissions to function:

  • storage: This permission is used to enable the Extension to function properly within Chrome. We do not use this permission to store any personal data or uploaded images.

5. Third-Party Services

The Icon Generator Extension does not integrate with or use any third-party services, APIs, or libraries that would collect or process your data.

6. Data Retention

Since all processing occurs locally and no data is transmitted to external servers:

  • No user data is stored by us
  • All uploaded images and generated icons exist only in your browser's temporary memory during processing
  • Once you close the Extension or your browser, any undownloaded data is automatically cleared

7. Children's Privacy

Our Extension is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since our Extension does not collect any personal information from any users, this includes children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Chrome Web Store and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, delete, or transfer. All data processing occurs locally on your device and is under your control.

10. Contact Us

11. Compliance with Legal Requirements

The Icon Generator Extension is designed to comply with privacy laws and regulations, including:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Because we do not collect, store, or process any personal information, our Extension maintains compliance with these regulations by design.

This Privacy Policy was created to be transparent about our data practices and to comply with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.

Privacy Policy

Second Home Button At Top Right

Last Updated: April 13, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Second Home Button At Top Right" Chrome extension ("we", "our", or "extension") collects, uses, and protects any information gathered when you use our extension.

Information Collection and Use

Our extension collects and stores the following information:

  • User-defined URLs and page links: These are the custom links you configure through the options page.
  • Extension settings and preferences: Any customization options you select for the extension's functionality.

All this information is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API and is not transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

Data Storage

We use Chrome's storage API to save your settings and preferences. This information is stored locally in your browser and persists between browser sessions. This ensures your custom links remain available when you restart your browser.

Data Sharing and Transfer

Your data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third parties. We do not collect, transmit, or store your browsing history, personal information, or any other data beyond what is required for the extension's core functionality.

Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage mechanisms, which provide appropriate security for this type of local data.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can access, modify, or delete this information at any time through the extension's options page. Uninstalling the extension will remove all stored data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Chrome Web Store listing for the extension. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Privacy Policy for JIRA Link Creator

Last Updated: April 21, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the JIRA Link Creator Chrome extension ("we," "our," or "the extension") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our extension. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and that you are fully informed about how your data is handled.

Information Collection and Use

The JIRA Link Creator extension is designed to operate entirely within your browser and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. Here's what you should know about our data practices:

Data Stored Locally

The extension stores the following configuration data using Chrome's storage.sync API:

  • Pattern settings (regular expression for matching JIRA ticket references)
  • URL template for generating links
  • Extension enabled/disabled status
  • List of excluded domains where the extension shouldn't operate

This data is stored on your device and synchronized across your Chrome instances through your Google account if you are signed in to Chrome. This data is used solely to provide the extension's functionality.

Website Content Access

The extension operates by scanning text content on web pages you visit to identify and convert JIRA-like references to clickable links. This scanning:

  • Only occurs on the client side (within your browser)
  • Does not save or transmit the content of pages you visit
  • Only processes text to identify patterns matching your configured JIRA reference format

Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

  • storage: To save your configuration preferences
  • activeTab: To access the content of your currently active tab to detect and convert JIRA references
  • tabs: To identify the current website for the site exclusion feature

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your browsing history
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Send any of your data to external servers

Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

Your Choices

You have complete control over the extension's functionality:

  • You can disable the extension globally or for specific sites
  • You can uninstall the extension at any time
  • You can customize the pattern and URL template

Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with or use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

The JIRA Link Creator extension does not specifically target or collect information from children under 13. The extension functions the same for all users regardless of age.

International Data Transfers

Since no data is collected or transmitted to external servers, there are no international data transfers.

Consent
